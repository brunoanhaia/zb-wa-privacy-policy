Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Zeta Tech ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects the personal information of users ("you" or "your") who interact with our WhatsApp bot, operated through the WhatsApp Business API.

By using our WhatsApp bot, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

We collect the following information when you interact with our WhatsApp bot:

  • Contact Information: Your WhatsApp phone number. We may collect your WhatsApp profile name if it's provided by you or by WhatsApp.
  • Message Content: The content of the messages you send to and receive from our bot. This may include text, images, audio, video, and other media types, depending on the bot's functionality.
  • Interaction Data: Information about how you interact with the bot, such as the commands you use, the options you select, and the frequency of your interactions.
  • Technical Information: Automatically generated data, such as message timestamps and session identifiers.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Operate the Bot: To answer your questions, process your requests, and provide the services or information you seek through the bot.
  • For Communication: To send you relevant messages about our services, or to respond to support requests.
  • For Legal Compliance and Security: To comply with legal and regulatory obligations, respond to legal processes, protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and to detect and prevent fraud and illegal activities.

3. How Do We Share Your Information?

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information with third parties in the following situations:

  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if we are required to do so by law, court order, or governmental request, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, safety, or property, or the safety of others.

4. Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose of processing.

5. Information Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. Therefore, while we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Our bot operates through the WhatsApp Business API, a platform provided by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.). When you interact with our bot, your messages and information are processed by WhatsApp's servers, subject to WhatsApp's terms of service and privacy policy. We encourage you to review WhatsApp's privacy policy to understand how they handle your data.
